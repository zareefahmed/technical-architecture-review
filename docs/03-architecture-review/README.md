# 3. Architecture Review

An architecture review is a structured look at a system's important decisions, before, during, or after they are made, to check whether they actually serve the requirements, the constraints, and the organization that has to live with them. This section covers why reviews matter, how they run, the methods available, and what you get out of them.

Review is worth doing even when everyone involved is competent and honest, because the person who made a decision is the person least able to see its blind spots. It becomes essential when some of the people involved have a commercial stake in the outcome. That situation gets its own full treatment in [Section 4: Independent Third-Party Review](../04-independent-third-party-review/README.md).
